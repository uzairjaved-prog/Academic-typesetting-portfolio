\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
This thesis is a demonstration of scalable LaTeX dissertation architecture. Its subject is the relationship between ambient temperature and electrical power demand across three monitoring sites, studied on \Nobs{} hourly observations from a \emph{synthetic} dataset. A hinge regression model and a feedback-control interpretation are developed, and every figure and table is regenerated by script from raw data. The document is split into one file per chapter, driven by a single preamble, with automatically generated contents, figure and table lists, bibliography and index.

\section{Least Squares}\index{least squares}
For a full-rank design matrix, ordinary least squares has a closed-form estimator. Its expectation and covariance follow from the linear model assumptions; a standard treatment is given by Kay~\cite{kay1993estimation}. The proposition in \cref{prop:ols} states these estimator properties. It does not select a particular regression model.

\begin{definition}[Hinge feature]
For a threshold $\tau$, the hinge feature of temperature $T$ is $h(T)=\max(T-\tau,0)$.
\end{definition}

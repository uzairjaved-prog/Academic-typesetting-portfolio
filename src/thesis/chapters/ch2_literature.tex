\section{Estimation Theory}\index{least squares}
For a linear model $\bm y=\bm X\bm\beta+\bm\varepsilon$ the least-squares estimator attains the Cram\'er--Rao bound under Gaussian noise~\cite{kay1993estimation}.

\begin{definition}[Hinge feature]
For threshold $\tau$ the hinge feature of temperature $T$ is $h(T)=\max(T-\tau,0)$.
\end{definition}

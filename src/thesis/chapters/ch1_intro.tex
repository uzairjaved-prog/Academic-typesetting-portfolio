\chapter{Introduction}

\section{Motivation}
Energy-monitoring\index{energy monitoring} systems record measurements over time. Temperature can be one of several factors associated with power demand\index{power demand}. This sample uses synthetic data\index{synthetic data} and generated hourly data to show how such measurements can be summarized and typeset. The values are illustrative; they do not come from a live site or a published experiment.

\section{Questions}
The example is organized around three questions:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{RQ\arabic*.}]
  \item How are temperature and power associated in the generated data?
  \item How do the three example sites differ in their generated power values?
  \item How can a feedback-control system be illustrated with a block diagram and a step-response plot?
\end{enumerate}

\section{Structure}
\Cref{ch:lit} describes the software and background, \cref{ch:method} explains the data and model, and \cref{ch:results} reports the summaries produced by the scripts. \Cref{ch:concl} states what these examples do and do not show.
